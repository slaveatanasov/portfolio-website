## The idea

Pregnancy is nine months of small announcements and decisions that families want to share: the reveal, the name shortlist, the due-date guesses. Most of that lives in group chats and generic poll apps. Next 9 Months gave it a dedicated, friendly home.

## What I built

- **Gender reveals** that a couple could share as a link and open together with family and friends.
- **Polls** around the pregnancy, with shareable links and a clean results view.
- **Baby-name finder** for browsing and shortlisting names.
- **Sharing first**: link previews and link-based access, so invitees could take part without friction.

## Stack and decisions

Next.js for the mix of static marketing pages and dynamic, link-driven app routes, with a deliberately small backend so one person could run the whole product. The focus was on shipping a complete, polished consumer experience end to end: brand, UI, data model, deployment and analytics.

## Outcome

Shipped end to end as a solo build. The product is no longer operating; the screenshots are from the live version. More screens and usage data will be added here.
